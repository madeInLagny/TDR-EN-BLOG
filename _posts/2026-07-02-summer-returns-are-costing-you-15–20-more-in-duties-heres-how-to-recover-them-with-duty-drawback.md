**Why the Spike?**
- **Bracketing**: Shoppers order multiple sizes/colors (common in apparel), intending to return the rest.
- **Seasonal Trends**: Summer fashion (swimwear, lightweight clothing) has **higher return rates** due to fit and style preferences.
